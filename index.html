How it works

From Idea to Publication

Every paper follows the same path: an open conversation, focused collaboration and a structured, citable publication.

  1. Stage 1

    Kitchen Ideas

    Raw hypotheses are shared and discussed in a moderated community, where early questions are welcome.

  2. Stage 2

    Study Groups

    Approved, private groups take a promising idea and focus on one goal: building the paper.

  3. Stage 3

    The Library

    The final work is published with structured metadata: abstract, DOI, authors and thematic axes.

Study Groups

How to Join a Study Group

Study Groups are private and moderated. Access is always granted through an application, so every group stays focused and respectful.

  1. 01

    Apply through the form

    Tell us about your background and which research question you want to work on.

  2. 02

    A moderator reviews it

    A moderator checks the fit with an active group, usually within a few working days.

  3. 03

    You receive a private invitation

    Once approved, the moderation team sends your invitation by e-mail.
